# Locked Product Decisions

## Branding

- Product name: PostMotive
- Customer-facing AI assistant: Ask Motive
- Visual direction: bright, smooth, premium, approachable
- Avoid the earlier black/red direction
- Design for novice marketers

## Product position

- AI Marketing Director, not a generic AI writer
- Guided questions instead of expecting prompts
- One-click workflows where safe
- Core success is completed marketing work, not generated text volume

## Libraries

- Media Library and Content Library are foundational
- Folder organization is required
- Completed media and content should be saved automatically by the relevant workflows

## Video

- TikTok/Reels short-form is a priority
- Default output should be a genuinely created short video, not only a text concept
- Cost must be controlled
- Credit reservation/refund must be reliable
- Users should be able to edit before publishing

## Compliance

- Industry selection occurs during onboarding
- Content and recommendations adapt by industry
- Risky content should be flagged with safer alternatives
- The product does not promise legal compliance

## Administrative accounts

Historical intended roles:

- Super Admin for founders with unlimited/billing-exempt access
- Demo account with sample data and reset capability

Verify implementation before relying on either.

## Removed/deprioritized dashboard areas

CRM and Inventory were previously removed from the primary dashboard. Do not reintroduce them without a clear product decision.
